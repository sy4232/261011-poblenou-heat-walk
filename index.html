What you are looking at

A computer simulation of one clear, hot summer day (30 July) in the Poblenou superblock in Barcelona.

  • At 1.1 m: a see-through sheet at the height of a person's body, showing how hot the street feels as a feels-like temperature (PET, °C). PET combines air temperature, sun and shade, heat radiated by surfaces, humidity and wind. Comfortable 18–23 °C, slightly warm 23–29, warm 29–35, hot 35–41, very hot above 41.
  • Surface temperature: the temperature of the pavement, walls and roofs from the simulation. Wind shows the wind speed at 1.1 m, with moving streaks for its direction.
  • Scenarios at the top left compare the street with and without trees. Tour walks through the main results in three steps.
  • Click any point on the map to see its numbers. Open Help for the controls.

Prototype for discussion. It shows a new simulation of 30 July 2026 in the Poblenou superblock with the existing street trees (2026-10 run, after two days of spin-up; the earlier version is published in Yamada et al. 2026, Street Art & Urban Creativity 12(5), doi:10.66838/sauc.6427). Known limits: the street air runs about 4 °C warm at midday compared with street measurements, so the feels-like temperature is likely too high; the open edge of the model area is not shown; the scenarios without trees and with reflective roads are still being computed.

Data sources: aerial photo © Institut Cartogràfic i Geològic de Catalunya (ICGC), ortofoto 25 cm 2025; street trees: Ajuntament de Barcelona, Open Data BCN (Arbrat viari, Arbrat zona); building footprints: Dirección General del Catastro; weather forcing: Servei Meteorològic de Catalunya (Meteocat). Simulation and viewer: NYU, UPC and Hochschule Mainz.
